FivexL, sure sounds cool
but what does it stand for?

League

Being a solo consultant is lonely. We band together so we can support each other, achieve more, and open doors that stay closed to solopreneurs and freelancers. Nobody solves a hard problem alone: another senior engineer reviews the design, and what one of us learns, the rest of us can use.

Location-independent

We have no office and no commute. Our team spans many countries, and our customers are mostly in the US and Europe. We bring good work to talented people where they already live, so nobody has to move to a big city and trade their quality of life for a career. Everyone chooses where they work and how they plan their day, and shows up for their team and their customers.

Lovely

Life is too short to work with people you don't like, trust and respect. Experts are rare, so we are picky and hire slowly: we are building a company we ourselves want to work in. It isn't the right company for everyone, but it is a great one for the people in it.

Lifetime Learners

This one matters most. Everyone here loves to learn and get better, and a team like that can go wherever the work goes. Today it is AWS; tomorrow it may be something else, and we will learn it and master it. Along the way we turn what we learn into tools and blueprints we can reuse.
