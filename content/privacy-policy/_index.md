This notice explains how FivexL Consulting Services OÜ handles the personal data you send us through the forms on this website: the newsletter sign-up, the contact and quote request form, webinar registration, webinar recording requests, and guide downloads.

By subscribing to our newsletter, you consent to FivexL Consulting Services OÜ processing your email to send updates, follow-ups, and occasional offers. You can unsubscribe anytime.

**Data Controller:** FivexL Consulting Services OÜ

**Contact:** hello@fivexl.io

## Email Subscription Privacy Notice (short form)

### What we collect

Your work email address (required) and the date you subscribed. We do not intentionally collect other personal data through this form.

### Why we process your data (purposes & legal basis)

- To send you updates, follow-ups, and relevant offers about FivexL consulting services (legal basis: your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
- Internal analytics to improve our content and services (consent).

### How we use your data

- Email delivery and subscription management.
- Basic engagement metrics (e.g., delivered/undelivered, unsubscribed).
- Your email and subscription date are also recorded in our customer relationship management (CRM) system, so our team can see who follows our updates.
- We do not sell your data.

### Retention

Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, or after a period of sustained inactivity (e.g., 24 months) unless required longer by law.

## Contact and Quote Request Form

### What we collect

Your full name, work email address, the project description you write, and which page you sent the form from.

### Why we process your data (purposes & legal basis)

- To reply to your request and discuss how we could work together (legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- To follow up on your request and keep a record of our conversations (legal basis: our legitimate interest in managing enquiries and client relationships, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

### How we use your data

- Your details and message are recorded in our CRM system, where our team handles enquiries.
- We do not add you to our newsletter unless you subscribe separately, and we do not sell your data.

### Retention

We keep your enquiry for as long as needed to respond to it and, if we work together, for the duration of our business relationship, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Webinar Registration, Webinar Recordings and Guide Downloads

### What we collect

- **Webinar registration:** your first name, last name, work email address, the webinar you registered for and when, whether you ticked the optional newsletter box, and, if you came from a link that contains them, the campaign tags in that link (such as which channel or campaign it came from).
- **Webinar recording requests:** your first name, last name, work email address, the webinar whose recording you asked for and when, whether you ticked the optional newsletter box, and the campaign tags in the link you came from, if it contains them.
- **Guide downloads:** your first name, last name, work email address, and which guide you downloaded and when.

### Why we process your data (purposes & legal basis)

- To register you for the webinar and send you the event details and the recording, or to give you the recording or guide you asked for (legal basis: providing the service you requested, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
- To understand which webinars, guides and campaigns people are interested in, so we can improve our content (legal basis: our legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- If you tick the optional newsletter box when registering for a webinar or asking for a recording, to send you our newsletter as described above (legal basis: your consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

### How we use your data

- Webinar registrations and recording requests are recorded in our CRM system, where our team can see which webinars you registered for or watched the recording of. They are also recorded in Brevo.
- Guide downloads are recorded in Brevo and in our CRM system, where our team can see which guides you downloaded.
- We do not sell your data.

### Retention

We keep your details for as long as needed for the webinar or guide you signed up for and to follow up on it, unless a longer period is required by law, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

## Service providers, sharing & transfers

We share your data only with trusted processors that help us provide these services:

- **Brevo**: email delivery, newsletter subscription management, webinar registrations, webinar recording requests and guide downloads.
- **Attio**: our CRM system, which stores newsletter subscribers, contact requests, webinar registrations, recording requests and guide downloads.
- **Cloudflare**: website hosting and spam protection. To block automated spam, Cloudflare Turnstile checks technical information from your browser when you send a form (legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping our forms free of abuse, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

If data is transferred outside the EEA, we use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

## Your rights

- Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability.
- Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us).
- Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority; in Estonia: the Data Protection Inspectorate.

## Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

## Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: hello@fivexl.io
