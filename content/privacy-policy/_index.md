## Company identification with Clay

Clay runs only after you click **Accept all** in the cookie banner. If you choose **Only necessary**, Clay is not loaded and none of the data below is collected.

When it runs, Clay uses:

- your IP address;
- the pages you visit, time on each page, the referring page, and UTM parameters in the link;
- browser and device characteristics: canvas, WebGL, installed fonts, audio, screen and hardware details. Together these form a device fingerprint.

Clay matches this data against its data providers to find the **company** you visit from, for example "a visitor from Example Ltd read the pricing page". We use this for sales and marketing: to see which companies are interested in our services. It works at company level. We do not use it to identify you as an individual.

Clay Labs Inc. acts as our processor. It stores data in the USA (AWS, us-east-1) and keeps each IP address lookup result for 30 days.

We make no automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Cookies and browser storage

We store our banner choice on every visit. Everything from Clay is set only after you click **Accept all**.

| Name | Where | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fivexl_consent` | localStorage | FivexL | Remembers your banner choice. Necessary: set on either choice | 12 months |
| `claydar_device_id`, `claydar_session`, `claydar_user_traits` | Cookies and localStorage | Clay | Visitor and session identifiers | Up to 12 months |
| `radar_sn_*` | sessionStorage | Clay | Cached device fingerprint hashes | 24 hours |
| `__sn_tld_probe` | Cookie | Clay | Finds which domain cookies can be set on | Deleted immediately |

Cloudflare Turnstile may set Cloudflare cookies on pages with a form. They are needed for spam protection.

Device fingerprinting reads your browser's characteristics instead of storing a file. It also runs only after **Accept all**.

**To change your choice**, click **Cookie settings** in the site footer. This reopens the banner. If you withdraw consent, Clay stops on the next page load and its browser storage is removed.
