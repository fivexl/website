{{< two_columns heading="What we do today" >}}
{{< column >}}
### AWS for health tech

This is our established work. We build and run AWS infrastructure for startups and scale-ups with sensitive data, most of them health tech companies that handle patient data under HIPAA.

Every project starts from our own blueprints: Right Start, a productized AWS landing zone, and an ECS blueprint for running containers in production. Security comes first in every design. We build the controls our customers' compliance work needs; the audit itself stays with their auditors.
{{< /column >}}

{{< column >}}
### AI forward-deployed engineering

This is our newer work. FivexL is a services partner in Anthropic's Claude Partner Network.

Our engineers join customer teams to bring AI agents and AI features into production, on the same secure AWS foundations we build for everyone else.
{{< /column >}}
{{< /two_columns >}}

{{< two_columns heading="How we work" >}}
{{< column >}}
### Where and how

**Remote only.** We have no office. The company is incorporated in Estonia.

**Full-time contractors.** You invoice us through your own company or through Deel.

**Anywhere with overlap.** Work from anywhere with at least 4 hours' overlap with Central European Time. Some roles also need overlap with the US East Coast.

**English** is our working language.
{{< /column >}}

{{< column >}}
### What we expect

**Flexibility with accountability.** You decide how and when to do the work, and you own the result.

**One cloud, one stack.** We work on AWS with Terraform and ECS. Doing the same kind of work many times is how we get better at it each time.
{{< /column >}}

{{< simple_section heading="Open source" >}}
We publish what we build for ourselves: more than 40 public repositories on [github.com/fivexl](https://github.com/fivexl). A few we use on most projects:

- [cloudtrail-to-slack](https://github.com/fivexl/terraform-aws-cloudtrail-to-slack) (100+ stars): alerts in Slack for the AWS CloudTrail events you care about.
- [sso-elevator](https://github.com/fivexl/terraform-aws-sso-elevator): temporary AWS access, requested and approved in Slack.
- [lprobe](https://github.com/fivexl/lprobe): health checks inside container images for ECS and Docker.
- [ec2-spot-price](https://github.com/fivexl/terraform-aws-ec2-spot-price): the current EC2 Spot price, always fresh.
- [account-baseline](https://github.com/fivexl/terraform-aws-account-baseline): a secure starting configuration for every AWS account.
- [ecs-events-to-slack](https://github.com/fivexl/terraform-aws-ecs-events-to-slack): ECS events delivered to Slack.

[See all our repositories](https://github.com/orgs/fivexl/repositories).
